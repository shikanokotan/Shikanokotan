# Noko Shikanoko &nbsp;·&nbsp; Arts Yang

**Principal Hardware & Systems Architect · NVIDIA**

---

I architect large-scale AI infrastructure. I created the original **NVIDIA Cloud Partner (NCP) Reference Designs** and authored the **NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD Reference Architectures** covering H200, B200, B300, GB200, GB300, VR NVL8, and VR NVL72.

Previously Principal Product Architect and Senior Systems Solution Architect at NVIDIA, and the responsible architect for **[NVIDIA Cambridge-1](https://www.nvidia.com/en-gb/industries/healthcare-life-sciences/cambridge-1/)** — the UK's most powerful AI supercomputer at its launch.

Before NVIDIA, I was a research staff and doctoral candidate at **Technische Universität München (TUM)** in association with the **Leibniz Supercomputer Centre (LRZ)**, funded by the *Bundesministerium für Bildung und Forschung* (BMBF). I earned my doctorate in Computer Science from TUM in 2020. Prior to my PhD I worked as a systems and software engineer at **Airbus Defence and Space** and IT Operations at **UnternehmerTUM gGmbH**.
